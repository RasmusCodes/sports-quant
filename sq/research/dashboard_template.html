How to read this

Market probabilities have the margin removed: Kalshi uses the mid of both team books, Polymarket its single book, and sportsbook moneylines are devigged with Shin's method. Model probabilities are produced strictly before each game (ratings use results of earlier days only; injury reports only if published before the decision time). Log loss and calibration, not betting returns, are the yardstick. The strategy results are a stress test of that yardstick under fees and executable prices. Closing-line value (CLV) is the less noisy measure of edge.
